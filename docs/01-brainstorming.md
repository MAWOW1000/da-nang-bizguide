# Brainstorming Notes

## Best Project Idea

Build Da Nang BizGuide as a trusted regulatory navigator, not just a chatbot.

The strongest master-project idea is:

An AI-powered company formation assistant for Da Nang that combines source-grounded regulatory guidance, human-reviewed legal knowledge, and blockchain-based proof of information integrity.

This gives the project three strong academic pillars:

- AI: natural-language guidance, chatbot, RAG, multilingual support.
- Blockchain: tamper-evident registry for source versions and approved knowledge.
- Public service / e-government: better access to business registration information.

## Target Users

Primary users:

- Local Vietnamese entrepreneurs who want to open a small or medium company.
- Foreign investors who want to establish a company in Da Nang.
- Startup founders who need a step-by-step checklist.
- Students/researchers studying business registration or regulatory technology.

Secondary users:

- Legal consultants and business service providers.
- Investment promotion staff.
- Admin/reviewer users who maintain the knowledge base.

## Core User Problems

- "I do not know where to start."
- "I do not know which business type fits my case."
- "I do not know which documents I need."
- "I do not know which official website is correct."
- "I do not know if the information is still valid."
- "I am worried that AI may give me wrong legal guidance."

## Product Modules

MVP modules:

- Homepage with business setup entry points.
- Guided questionnaire for user scenario discovery.
- AI chatbot with source citations.
- Business setup checklist generator.
- Official source library.
- Admin review workflow.
- Blockchain proof viewer.

Advanced modules:

- Multilingual support: Vietnamese, English, Korean, Chinese, Japanese.
- AI agent that compares user situation with applicable procedure paths.
- AI marketplace or expert handoff where verified consultants can answer complex cases.
- Automatic source monitoring for changed legal pages.
- Knowledge graph of agencies, procedures, forms, business types, and legal documents.
- User workspace for saving checklists and notes.

## Why Blockchain Matters Here

Blockchain should solve a trust and audit problem.

Good blockchain use cases:

- Prove that a checklist was based on a specific approved knowledge version.
- Prove that a source snapshot existed before an answer was generated.
- Prove that an admin approved a regulatory update.
- Detect if a stored source document or knowledge version has been modified.
- Provide transparent version history for important regulatory guidance.

Weak blockchain use cases to avoid:

- Storing full legal documents on-chain.
- Storing personal documents on-chain.
- Using blockchain only as a database replacement.
- Adding crypto payments unless the thesis specifically needs it.

## Suggested Innovation

Most business-registration websites are static information pages. Most chatbots do not prove where their answers came from.

This project can be different by combining:

- RAG chatbot with citations.
- Human-reviewed official-source knowledge.
- Blockchain proof of knowledge integrity.
- Scenario-based business registration checklist.
- Evaluation of user trust and answer accuracy.

## Possible Thesis Contribution

The thesis can argue that AI guidance for regulatory procedures needs more than natural language generation. It needs an architecture for source grounding, update governance, and verifiable information provenance.

The contribution can be:

- A reference architecture for trusted AI regulatory guidance.
- A prototype for Da Nang company formation support.
- A smart contract design for knowledge version verification.
- An evaluation framework for chatbot answer accuracy, user trust, and blockchain verification.

## MVP Success Definition

The first successful demo should allow a user to:

1. Select a business scenario.
2. Receive a step-by-step company setup checklist.
3. Ask the chatbot a question.
4. See cited official-source references in the answer.
5. View the knowledge version and blockchain proof.
6. Understand that the system is guidance, not a substitute for official legal advice.
