# Official Source Candidates

This file lists candidate official sources for the Da Nang BizGuide knowledge base. Every source should be reviewed before being used in trusted chatbot answers.

Last initial review: 2026-06-19

## Primary Official Sources

### National Business Registration Portal

URL: https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/

Purpose:

- Enterprise registration portal.
- Business registration information.
- Online business registration services.
- Business registration office information by province/city.

Use in project:

- Company registration guidance.
- Official portal links.
- Source snapshots for business registration procedures.

### National Public Service Portal

URL: https://dichvucong.gov.vn/

Purpose:

- National gateway for public administrative services.

Use in project:

- Official service links.
- Cross-checking business-related administrative procedures.

### Da Nang Public Service / 1022 Portal

URL: https://1022.vn/

- Da Nang public service information, contact center, and public service support.

- Local administrative guidance source candidates.

## Source Governance Rules

- Prefer official government sources over private legal-service articles.
- Store source URL, retrieval date, page title, document date, and reviewer.
- Capture source snapshots before generating approved knowledge.
- Keep extracted summaries separate from original source snapshots.
- Require reviewer approval before content is used by the chatbot.
- Register approved knowledge version hashes on blockchain.
- Re-check time-sensitive legal content before thesis submission and final demo.

## Useful Source Metadata

For every source, collect:

- Source id
- Title
- URL
- Agency/owner
- Language
- Retrieval date
- Effective date if available
- Last updated date if available
- Procedure category
- Extracted text hash
- Reviewer
- Review status
- Blockchain version id if approved
